\addchap{Notation}

The two literatures that this book translates disagree on almost every normalization. The chapters share the conventions below; where a source uses another, the notes at the end of the chapter translate it. A chapter that needs a clashing local symbol says so where it introduces it.

\addsec*{Sizes and Gaussian notation}

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.27\textwidth}p{0.69\textwidth}@{}}
$N$ & the number of spins or variables of a spin-glass or matrix model \\
$d$, $n$, $\alpha=n/d$ & the dimension of the unknown in a learning problem, the number of samples or patterns, and the load \\
$Z$, $z\sim\Normal(0,1)$ & a standard Gaussian variable; $\Dz$ is the standard Gaussian measure, so $\int f(z)\Dz=\E f(Z)$ \\
$\phi$, $\Phi$, $H$ & the standard normal density and distribution function, and $H(x)=\Prob(Z>x)=1-\Phi(x)$ \\
$\gibbs{\cdot}$, $\E$ & the Gibbs (posterior) average, and the average over the disorder (the data)
\end{tabular}

\addsec*{Free entropy, replicas and overlaps}

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.27\textwidth}p{0.69\textwidth}@{}}
$Z_N$ & the partition function; in inference, the evidence (marginal likelihood) \\
$\phi_N=\frac1N\E\log Z_N$, $\phi$ & the free entropy and its limit; the physicist's free energy is $f=-\phi/\beta$ \\
$\sigma^1,\sigma^2,\dots$ & replicas: independent samples from the same Gibbs measure \\
$R_{12}=\frac1N\ip{\sigma^1}{\sigma^2}$, $q$ & the overlap of two replicas, a random variable, and its limit or typical value \\
$m=\frac1N\ip{\sigma}{x^*}$ & the overlap with the truth (the magnetization, in the physicist's word)
\end{tabular}

\addsec*{The models}

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.27\textwidth}p{0.69\textwidth}@{}}
SK & $H_N(\sigma)=\frac\beta{\sqrt N}\sum_{i<j}g_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j+h\sum_i\sigma_i$, Gibbs weight $e^{H_N}$; covariance $N\xi(R)$ with $\xi(q)=\beta^2q^2/2$, so $\beta_c=1$ \\
REM & $Z_N=\sum_\sigma e^{\beta\sqrt N X_\sigma}$, $X_\sigma$ independent $\Normal(0,1)$; $\beta_c=\sqrt{2\log2}$ \\
spiked Wigner & $A=\frac\lambda Nxx^\top+W$, $W$ from the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble with off-diagonal variance $1/N$; the BBP transition is at $\lambda=1$ \\
scalar channel & $Y=\sqrt\gamma X+Z$; in spiked Wigner $\gamma=\lambda^2q$ \\
perceptron & patterns $g_\mu\sim\Normal(0,I_d)$, weights $\norm w^2=d$ or $w\in\{\pm1\}^d$, constraints $\ip{g_\mu}{w}/\sqrt d\ge\kappa$ (the margin $\kappa$) \\
GLM & $y_\mu=\varphi_{\mathrm{out}}(\ip{x_\mu}{w^*}/\sqrt d,A_\mu)$ with $x_\mu\sim\Normal(0,I_d)$ \\
regression & $x\sim\Normal(0,\Sigma)$, $y=\ip{x}{\beta^*}+\sigma\varepsilon$, $r^2=\ip{\beta^*}{\Sigma\beta^*}$; ridge $\argmin\frac1{2n}\norm{y-X\beta}^2+\frac\lambda2\norm\beta^2$; excess risk $R$ \\
spherical $p$-spin & $\E H_N(\sigma)H_N(\sigma')=N R(\sigma,\sigma')^p$ on the sphere of radius $\sqrt N$; threshold energy $-E_\infty=-2\sqrt{(p-1)/p}$; physicists' energies are these divided by $\sqrt2$
\end{tabular}

\addsec*{Status words}

The status boards use seven words: \emph{proved} (a theorem in a refereed publication), \emph{proved under} (with hypotheses beyond the prediction, which the board names), \emph{preprint} (proved only in an unrefereed preprint), \emph{conjectured} (with the evidence), \emph{heuristic} (an argument that claims no exactness), \emph{known false} (with the counterexample), and \emph{open}.

\addsec*{Probability and asymptotics}

$\Prob$ and $\E$ without a subscript are over the disorder; a subscript names other randomness. $\Normal(\mu,\sigma^2)$ is the normal distribution and $\Unif$ the uniform one. Limits are taken as the sizes grow at fixed ratios (the proportional limit) unless a chapter says otherwise, and every limit statement says in what sense it holds. Logarithms are natural.
